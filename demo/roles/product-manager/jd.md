# Senior Product Manager — Job Description

We are hiring a Senior Product Manager to own our analytics product line.

## Requirements

- 6+ years product management, B2B SaaS preferred
- Experience owning a product roadmap and shipping on cadence
- Customer discovery: interviews, surveys, usage analytics (SQL)
- Pricing and packaging experience
- Cross-functional leadership with engineering and design
- Data-driven decision making (A/B testing, metrics)

## Preferred

- Developer tools / API products
- PLG or usage-based pricing experience
- Experience with 3+ person PM team leadership

## Responsibilities

- Own strategy and roadmap for the analytics product
- Run continuous discovery with enterprise customers
- Define and track success metrics (activation, retention, revenue)
- Partner with engineering on technical tradeoffs and API design
